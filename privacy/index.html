The short version

  • We only collect what you send us through the contact form, by email or on WhatsApp.
  • We use it only to reply to you. We never sell it or use it for marketing.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time: adewoyefoundation@gmail.com.

Who we are

The Joseph Alabi Adewoye Foundation ("the foundation", "we", "us") is a non-governmental organisation registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria under number RC 8401335. We are the data controller for personal information collected through adewoyefoundation.org.

Address: Iji Road, opposite Ijara-Isin Town Hall, Ijara-Isin, Kwara State, Nigeria. Email: adewoyefoundation@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us, plus the basic technical information any website needs to work.

  • When you use the contact form: your first name, last name, email address and message, together with anything you include in the message.
  • When you email us or message us on WhatsApp: your name, contact details and whatever you send. WhatsApp messages are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • When you visit the site: our hosting provider automatically records standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Please don't include sensitive personal information (for example health, religious or financial details) in your message unless it's necessary.

How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • reply to your enquiry and continue the conversation you started;
  • send you an automatic confirmation that we received your message;
  • keep the website secure and working properly; and
  • meet any legal obligations that apply to us.

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we rely on your consent when you choose to contact us, on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and keeping the website secure, and on legal obligation where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools of any kind, and it doesn't store anything in your browser. Fonts and icons are served from our own website, so your visit isn't shared with font or content-delivery services.

Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for marketing. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the website, who may process it solely on our instructions:

  • Netlify: hosts the website and processes server logs.
  • Resend: delivers contact-form messages and confirmation emails.
  • Google (Gmail): the email service where we receive and reply to your messages.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of the foundation or others.

International transfers

Our service providers are based outside Nigeria, including in the United States, so your information may be processed there. We use established providers that apply recognised data-protection safeguards to the information they handle, and we only share what's needed for the purposes above.

How long we keep it

We keep contact-form messages and email correspondence only as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and keep a reasonable record of our communications, and then we delete them. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a short period for security purposes.

How we protect it

The website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and access to messages is limited to the people at the foundation who need it to respond. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it;
  • ask for your information in a portable format; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything we did before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these rights, email adewoyefoundation@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we'll respond within the time required by law.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng). We'd appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

Children

This website isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 through it. If you believe a child has sent us their information, please contact us at adewoyefoundation@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

Links to other websites

This website links to other sites and services, such as WhatsApp and our web developer's website. We're not responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their policies when you visit them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.

Contact us

For any questions about this policy or your personal information, email adewoyefoundation@gmail.com or write to us at Iji Road, opposite Ijara-Isin Town Hall, Ijara-Isin, Kwara State, Nigeria.
