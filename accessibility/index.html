Our commitment

The Joseph Alabi Adewoye Foundation is committed to making this website accessible. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, the internationally recognised standard for accessible websites.

What we've done

This website has been designed and built so that you can:

  • navigate every page using only a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link;
  • use a screen reader, with headings, landmarks and labelled controls in a logical order;
  • zoom in up to 200% and resize text without content being cut off;
  • read text with sufficient colour contrast against its background;
  • understand images through text alternatives;
  • turn off animation: if your device is set to reduce motion, the website's animations are switched off; and
  • read all content and send us a message even if JavaScript is turned off.

How we test

We checked the website on 2 November 2026 using automated testing (axe-core) and manual checks with a keyboard and at different zoom levels and screen sizes. We'll keep testing as the website changes.

Known limitations

We're aware of the following:

  • Some photographs are historical family archives. Their text descriptions identify who and what is shown, but don't describe every detail.
  • Pages and services we link to, such as WhatsApp, are run by other organisations and may not meet the same standard.

Compatibility

The website is designed to work with recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile, and with common screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver and TalkBack.

Feedback and contact

If you have difficulty using any part of this website, or need information in a different format, please email adewoyefoundation@gmail.com. Tell us the page and the problem you found, and we'll do our best to fix it or get you the information another way.
